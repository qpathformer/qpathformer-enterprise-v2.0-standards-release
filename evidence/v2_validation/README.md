# V2 Validation and Editorial Evidence

This folder is populated with dated technical and manuscript-verification
records retained for the v2.0 Standards Edition. It is not an empty output
placeholder or a single combined experiment.

The [initial local slice](standards_local_20260905/) and
[consolidation record](standards_consolidation_20260905/) identify their own
source selections and checks. Other dated folders retain editorial checks,
paper-alignment observations and the separate
[Windows symlink recheck](windows_symlink_recheck_20260905/).
The [Windows dry-run unit log](windows_dryrun_20260905_unit_tests.txt) is also
preserved as its own observation.

Read each record's date, source identity, environment and limitations. An older
test count, skip or manuscript hash remains evidence of its original object;
it is not replaced by a later PASS or by the final PDF identities. References
to earlier manuscript and QA files do not imply those files are bundled.

For a new run, use the [local checks](../../docs/execution/LOCAL_CHECKS.md) and
keep new reports outside the final release selection unless separately
reviewed. Do not overwrite these retained records. Use the
[provenance index](../../release/provenance/README.md) for source and historical
locators. Evidence presence does not grant training, scoring, sharing or
publication authority.
