# Private Baseline Preparation Records

This folder is populated with retained preparation checks, environment details
and unit-test logs from the private development baseline. The folder name and
original records are preserved for provenance; they do not designate the whole
v2.0 Standards Edition as a private-only publication.

- [Local preparation validation](LOCAL_PREPARATION_VALIDATION.json) and
  [workspace validation](WORKSPACE_VALIDATION.json) describe their recorded
  preparation snapshots.
- [Baseline precommit check](PRIVATE_BASELINE_PRECOMMIT_CHECK.json) and
  [Git preparation check](GIT_PREPARATION_CHECK.json) describe their named
  checks, not an independently established public commit, tag or release.
- [Source preservation check](SOURCE_PRESERVATION_CHECK.json),
  [environment record](VALIDATION_ENVIRONMENT.json) and retained unit logs
  preserve the corresponding identities and observations.

These are not a seal of the current standards folder, a new release approval
or an authorization for an operation. Their original hashes, paths and test
results must not be rewritten to match later runs.

Use the [provenance index](../provenance/README.md) to interpret the source
records and the [local checks](../../docs/execution/LOCAL_CHECKS.md) for a new
verification. Store fresh reports separately from the selected release until
reviewed; retain these historical records unchanged.
