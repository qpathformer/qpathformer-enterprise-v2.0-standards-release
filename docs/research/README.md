## Historical and Editorial Records

Word originals, earlier manuscripts, editorial ledgers and internal QA renderings are preserved locally outside the release. They are not additional publication papers. Their external locations and available identities are described in the finalization record and the [relocation index](../../release/provenance/RELOCATION_INDEX_20260906.json).

Earlier handoffs describe their own dated manuscripts and checks, not these final PDF bytes. References to them in the papers remain historical locators; they do not promise that every named companion is bundled. Do not substitute earlier Word or QA-PDF hashes for the registered final exports.

The [history notice](../../history_notice.md) and [portable provenance index](../../release/provenance/README.md) retain the evidence boundary. No manuscript, historical result or licence was changed by final PDF registration.
