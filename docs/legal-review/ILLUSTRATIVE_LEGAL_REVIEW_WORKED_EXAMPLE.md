## Purpose

This populated example shows the information a technical service can organize for a practitioner and how a bounded review summary can remain separate from operational authorization. It is not a signed legal opinion. It accompanies TR010 Section 13.4 and does not assess the real TR papers, standards extracts, code licences, models or historical rights schedule.

The source is a [four-statement synthetic fixture](../../tests/fixtures/synthetic/legal_review/materials.json). Its exact SHA-256 is recorded in [the illustrative review record](../../tests/fixtures/synthetic/legal_review/review_record.json). The hash identifies bytes; it does not establish ownership or a legal conclusion. Nothing in this example is inserted into a live registry or passed to the training authorization creator.

## Separate Organizational Decision

The hypothetical next step, after a real practitioner completes the scoped review and conditions are resolved, is a separate decision by the responsible organization for one named operation. It must refer to the reviewed material identity and current conditions. Neither a legal review nor an organizational decision creates missing third-party rights.

For this example, the actual organizational disposition is `NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_REAL_OPERATION`. There is no ALLOW artifact, signing key, training approval or publication permission. A narrative example cannot be upgraded by changing a flag to true or copying a fictional reviewer identifier into a real authorization.

The existing synthetic CLI rehearsal remains a separate control test. It starts from explicitly fictitious decisions and disposable keys and does not prove legal review. The current runtime does not consume this illustrative review record or enforce all the proposed conditions in it.

## AI and Professional Responsibility

AI assistance may organize an inventory, draft questions or suggest a record structure. The proposed profile does not accept AI output as the required practitioner review. A practitioner using AI remains responsible for independent judgment, verification and confidentiality, as described in the [joint Australian legal-regulator statement](https://www.lawsociety.com.au/sites/default/files/2024-12/LS4590_PSP_Statement_AI_LawSocietyVersion.pdf).

This division of work supports proposed services in rights mapping, exception resolution, licensing conditions and periodic reassessment. It is not a finding that lawyers are redundant or an offer of legal services by the technical platform.

## Replacing the Example With Real Evidence

A real record requires an actual engagement and appropriately qualified practitioner, identified materials and uses, relevant jurisdictions, independently assessed evidence, recorded conditions and unresolved exceptions, a date and reliance scope, and a separate organizational authorization. Keep an approved bounded summary in the technical package and confidential advice in a controlled matter system. Do not relabel this fictional record as historical proof that those steps occurred.
